New York City, NYC, NY

What makes New York special?

New York is an epicenter of global influence in finance and culture with a rich history that almost everyone is taught at some point in their life. New York is known as one of the most diverse states in the world, with there being a population of ~8.58 milllion people currently residing within the state.

How to be an honarary New Yorker

  1. Walk fast on the street- don't stop in the middle of the sidewalk and don't walk slowly- always look like you know where you're going
  2. Don't talk to strangers- don't fall for scams and definitely don't trust someone you've just met on the street- not in NYC
  3. Learn how to take public transit before anything- the trains are the heart of New York. If you can't take the trains, you can't get anywhere, really
  4. Be alert wherever you are- the train, the street, in a cab, etc. (everywhere, really- don't ever let your guard down)
  5. Keep in mind that a lot goes on in New York. Be as loud as you want, have as much fun as you can, and don't be afraid to take up space

New York is also extremely diverse!

As stated from nyc.gov, "NYC is a diverse and global city:

  • Home to a population that speaks over 200 languages
  • 37% of New Yorkers were born outside of the United States, and as many as 49% speak a language other than English at home
  • With public signage written in many different languages, NYC is making it easier for non-English speakers to live and work comfortably in the city
  • Each neighborhood in NYC has unique strengths and demographics, allowing businesses to find a borough and community to match their needs
  • Over three million New Yorkers are foreign-born, and almost 50% of the city's businesses are owned by immigrants"
